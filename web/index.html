How to read this figure

Color represents the percentage of examined shrimp in which a taxon was detected: positive shrimp ÷ examined shrimp × 100. The scale stays fixed at 0–100% in every view and palette. White is zero; grey is a missing record.

This workbook measures detection prevalence, not bacterial density or relative abundance. Several taxa can occur in one shrimp, so percentages across taxa need not sum to 100. The colored groups follow the supplied reference figure and are display groups, not formal taxonomic ranks.

HP is interpreted as hepatopancreas. Treatment codes and sampling labels are preserved from the workbook. Group totals and individual-shrimp profiles cannot be inferred from these summaries.
